About the Author

Born in Michigan and raised in Florida, Matthew Robert Zender draws on a vibrant background to infuse his characters with striking authenticity and emotional depth. His marriage to his husband—a native of Bogotá, Colombia—further inspires his work, informing its themes of acceptance, cultural intersection, and the beauty of varied perspectives.

Passionate about the intricate art of storytelling, Matthew is committed to amplifying diverse voices in contemporary fiction. His debut novel, Real Family, is a resonant coming-of-age story that explores profound personal transformation, the complex nature of human relationships, and the unexpected places we discover our true kin.
