Why Real Family is Perfect for Your Club

Real Family explores the profound personal transformation, the complex nature of human relationships, and the unexpected places we discover our true kin.

It's a story that sparks deep conversation about loyalty, identity, cultural intersection, and what it truly means to belong. With five distinctly different characters whose paths intertwine, your book club will find endless themes to discuss, relate to, and debate.

Virtual Q&A Sessions Available

I would love to join your book club! I am explicitly available for 30-to-60-minute virtual Q&A sessions via Zoom or Google Meet. No matter where your club is located, it's incredibly easy to connect and dive into the world of Real Family together.
