Muñeca
(moo – nyecka)

Five Lives. One Doll. An Unforgettable Legacy of Love and Fate Spanning Generations.

An ordinary keepsake becomes an extraordinary source of strength in this sweeping, multi-generational debut novel.

In a quiet Peruvian village, an elderly Quechua woman places the final, meticulous stitches on a handcrafted doll. Infusing the muñeca with materials from her own life and the rich heritage of her ancestry, she passes away peacefully, sending a piece of her spirit out into the wider world. When American tourists Nora and Jack Travis arrive, Nora’s deep intuition draws her inexplicably to the doll. Recognizing this profound connection, the maker’s grandson, Miguel, gifts it to her, sparking a decades-spanning journey across time and geography.

As the doll travels from owner to owner, including a determined young gymnast in Florida and an ambitious student navigating the bustling streets of Washington, D.C., each girl gives it a new name and a piece of her heart. The muñeca becomes a silent witness to five distinct lives, serving as a vital anchor of comfort, resilience, and cultural identity during their most pivotal, transformative chapters.

Through family struggles, personal growth, and romantic discovery, this single handcrafted doll serves as a profound vessel of fate, weaving together seemingly disparate lives into a beautiful, seamless narrative of unconditional love. Rich with cultural heritage and emotional depth, Muñeca builds to a poignant, full-circle revelation that underscores the enduring power of family and the invisible, unbreakable threads that tie us all together.
